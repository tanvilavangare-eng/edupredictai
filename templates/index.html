About The Project

Bridging Education and Predictive Artificial Intelligence

Traditional educational reporting relies on retrospective report cards issued after the semester concludes — when it is already too late to help a struggling student.

The AI-Based Student Performance Prediction System is built on the philosophy of proactive education. By continuously synthesizing five vital behavioral and academic pillars — Attendance %, Internal Assessments, Coursework Submissions, Prior Term Results, and Daily Study Hours — our trained Random Forest Regressor provides teachers with timely, transparent alerts.
